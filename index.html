About us

Who we are

Africa Centre for Climate Change and Environmental Sustainability (ACCES) is a woman youth-led collective empowering individuals, communities and initiatives on environmental stewardship.

We educate and empower marginalized communities, individuals, groups and school-going children with climate education and participatory learning through hands-on experiences and practices to promote conservation, restoration and sustainability.

We restore degraded school lands, bridge the climate information gap through climate education sessions and capacity building, and hold local authorities and leaders accountable for environmental commitments.

Our strategic vision is to build a climate-resilient environment while nurturing the young generation to promote policy and champion a climate-conscious world and protect our biodiversity. At our initiative, we promote a systems approach through our activities in land restoration, education and leadership to promote environmental change.

Thematic areas

Our focus areas

  • Climate justice: Through advocacy, empowerments, adaptation and mitigation strategies.
  • Biodiversity: Through protection of endangered species, ecosystems and environmental resilience.
  • Community empowerments and innovation: Involvement of indigenous and marginalized individuals including young people, women and girls and people with disabilities.
  • Education: Nurturing environmental awareness through climate education to bridge the climate literacy gap.
  • Restoration: Restoring degraded landscapes in urban and rural areas through afforestation and reforestation.
  • Marine conservation: Freshwater ecosystems preservation through advocacy campaigns, ocean education and mangrove restoration.
  • Pollution: Tackling plastic, organic and marine pollution through cleanups and promotion of appropriate waste disposal.
  • Renewable energy efficiency: Advocating for a just transition economy to promote advancement of clean and affordable renewable energy access.
  • Sustainable agriculture: Promoting sustainable mechanisms for agriculture and agribusiness practices for food security, reducing hunger and poverty.

Flagship Project

Restore School Lands for Sustainability

"Restore School Lands for Sustainability" addresses the challenge of climate information gap by educating learners and community members and integrating participatory learning through restoration hands-on practices.

This helps in turning the degraded school lands and environments into green spaces conducive for learning, hence supporting the well-being of those in the environments and its surroundings.

With our project, we build a connection to nature while promoting climate resilience, mitigation, and adaptation strategies on biodiversity protection, water conservation and effective waste management practices. We blend systemic change by integrating the restoration measures into learning environments.

When designing the activities, we collaborate and partner with the local authorities and the educators to ensure effectiveness and alignment with the climate and education goals and mission. With this approach, it is scalable and easy to replicate in other communities and regions, promoting impact.

Target Group / Beneficiaries

  • Young people, primary, junior and senior secondary school learners who will have the opportunity to receive climate education and hands-on practicals on restoration (ages 12-35).
  • Teachers and school administrators who will act as a bridge to climate education and integrate the modules even after the program.
  • Learning institutions, communities and environment for a safe and conducive learning environment through the restoration activities.
  • Local and surrounding communities benefiting from climate education awareness and biodiversity.
  • Youth climate leaders and volunteers who will be joining us in our activities.
