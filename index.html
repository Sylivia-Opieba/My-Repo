Our Mission

Building a resilient, sustainable Africa

ACCES promotes climate knowledge, environmental stewardship, and inclusive sustainability through advocacy, partnerships, and community engagement.

Climate Research

Investigating climate risks and promoting practical, evidence-based adaptation strategies.

Sustainable Development

Supporting environmentally responsible resource use in agriculture, energy, water, and biodiversity systems.

Community Action

Empowering people and institutions to respond to climate challenges with practical local solutions.
